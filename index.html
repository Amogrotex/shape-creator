Shape Creator

Pinch your fingers in the air to build 3D shapes, pull them bigger, drop them in the bin.

Camera is off

Everything runs on your device — no video ever leaves the browser.

How to play

  1. Create — pinch both hands (thumb + index) close together, then pull them apart.
  2. Resize — while still pinching with both hands, change the distance between them.
  3. Move — pinch near a shape with one hand and drag it around.
  4. Delete — drag a shape onto the bin (bottom right) and let go of the pinch.

Keys: C clear · Space add · F fullscreen · D diagnostics · H help

No webcam? Drag with the mouse / your finger — same rules apply.
