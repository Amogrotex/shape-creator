How to cast

  1. Draw — pinch your thumb and index finger, then move your hand to draw. Release the pinch to cast.
  2. Z — lightning: arcs to the nearest wisp and chains to the next one.
  3. ^ — fireball: flies at the nearest wisp and explodes, hitting everything nearby.
  4. O — shield: a bubble around the crystal that soaks up hits.

Tip: big, confident glyphs are recognised best — small scribbles fizzle. Keys: H help · R restart · P performance mode · F fullscreen.
